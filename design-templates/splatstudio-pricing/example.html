Frequently asked questions

How long is the free trial?

Seven days on the Free plan, with every Pro feature enabled. No credit card is required to start, and exports during the trial carry a small watermark.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month; you can cancel from billing settings and keep access until the end of the paid period.

How do seats work?

Editors consume a seat; viewers are always free. Studios teams can reassign seats when people change roles.

Do you offer yearly invoices?

Annual plans are billed once in EUR. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice with net-30 terms.

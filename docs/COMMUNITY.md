## Where to talk about what

- **Bugs / features** — open an issue in the repo.
- **Plugins** — community plugins live in `plugins/community/`; premium in
  `premium-ecosystem/premium_plugins/` (see
  [PLUGIN-ECOSYSTEM.md](./PLUGIN-ECOSYSTEM.md)).
- **Design systems / skills** — contributions follow
  `design-systems/` and `skills/` conventions ([skills ATTRIBUTION](../skills/ATTRIBUTION.md)).
- **General** — the subreddit and Discord above.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the free trial?

Seven days on the Free plan with Pro behavior enabled. No credit card is required to start.

How do subscriptions work?

Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. Pro and Studios are per seat; viewers are always free. Manage or cancel from the app's billing portal.

Do you offer yearly billing?

Yes — annual billing is roughly two months free. Studios customers can also pay by invoice.

What is the API key tier?

Pro and Studios include programmatic API access; the Free plan is UI-only.
